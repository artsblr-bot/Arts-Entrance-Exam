# Gurukul Arts — Advanced Round (100 Questions)
### Hard Tier | Trick Options | For Applicants Claiming Prior Knowledge

**Purpose:** The base exam checks if someone is teachable. This round checks if someone who *says* they already know the craft actually does. Every correct answer here is simple in principle — but the wrong options are built to sound just as correct. A confident guesser gets caught here. Someone who genuinely understands the "why" behind each answer sails through.

**How to use this round:** Don't give this to every applicant. Give it only to candidates who scored well on the base exam or who claim significant prior experience. Treat a strong score here as a green flag for a lead/senior role. Treat a weak score here (paired with a strong base exam) as completely fine — it just means "raw talent, needs training," which is exactly the kind of person you said you wanted.

**A note on design:** Every wrong option in this round was written to sound like something a half-informed person would confidently pick.
